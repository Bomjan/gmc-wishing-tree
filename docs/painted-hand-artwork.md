# Painted hand artwork v1

Asset: `public/handprints/painted-hand-v1.png` (1254 × 1254 PNG with transparency).

Created with the built-in ImageGen tool during implementation. The supplied handprint images informed the desired style; this is newly generated generic artwork, not a scan or extraction of any visitor's skin or fingerprint. No additional image-generation calls occur during visitor use. The source file is preserved as generated; Canvas 2D performs runtime colour tinting/mirroring and seeded paint wear.

This asset is versioned and must remain immutable for accepted contributions. Future artwork changes should use a new versioned asset and distinct persisted source presets.

## Generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production alpha texture for a museum touchscreen painted-handprint stamp. Generate ONE isolated black ink hand impression on a genuinely transparent background. A believable complete human palm print with exactly five fingers, palm heel, thumb extending to the LEFT side of the canvas, four fingers pointing up with a relaxed natural fan; middle finger longest, ring slightly shorter, index shorter than middle, pinky shortest. Rounded anatomically natural fingertips and palm, short wrist base. Like a real hand dipped in thin black printing ink and pressed onto glass: irregular broken pigment coverage, fine mottled dry ink grain, organic palm-crease voids crossing the palm diagonally and curving near the thumb, uneven rubbed edges, small negative gaps at finger joints. Rich mostly solid ink with many small transparent scratches and creases, readable overall full hand shape. Generic invented artistic skin crease texture, not an individual's biometric print; no detailed identifiable fingerprint whorls. 2D flat ink artwork, not a photograph of a hand, no three-dimensional skin, no shadows, no outlines around the print, no rectangular boxes or circles, no separate assembled geometric components. Square canvas, hand centred, full hand visible with comfortable equal transparent margins about 6 percent, keep every finger in frame. Ink is black; ALL unprinted paper areas, including every scratch and crease, must be transparent alpha rather than painted white. No white paper, no checkerboard background, no text, no watermark.
